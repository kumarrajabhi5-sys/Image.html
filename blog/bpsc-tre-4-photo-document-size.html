Why Does BPSC Set Such Strict File Size Limits?

The BPSC online portal has limited server capacity and processes lakhs of applications simultaneously. Strict file size limits ensure the portal doesn't crash under heavy load and that all applications are stored efficiently. This is standard practice across all Indian government exam portals including UPSC, SSC, and state PSC boards.

The good news is that reducing a passport photo from 3MB to 40KB is completely achievable without any visible quality loss — because at 200x230 pixels, the image is small anyway and doesn't need high resolution data.

What Quality Setting Should You Use For BPSC Photos?

For BPSC TRE 4 passport photos, use 55-65% JPG quality. At 200x230 pixel dimensions, this consistently produces files between 15KB and 45KB — well within the required range. The photo will still be perfectly clear for identification purposes.

For signatures and thumb impressions at 140x60 pixels, use 40-50% quality. Signatures are simple black and white images with minimal detail, so compression has almost no visible impact.

Common Mistakes Candidates Make

  • Uploading original camera photo: Smartphone photos are typically 2-8MB — 100x over the limit. Always resize and compress first.
  • Wrong dimensions: BPSC specifies exact pixel dimensions. A photo that is the right KB size but wrong dimensions may still be rejected.
  • Using PNG format: PNG files are larger than JPG at the same quality. Always save as JPG/JPEG for government forms.
  • Compressing too aggressively: Going below 30% quality can make your face unrecognisable. Stay between 50-70% for photos.
  • Using a blurry or old photo: Compress a recent, clear photo — not an old one that is already low quality.

How to Compress Documents for BPSC TRE 4

For certificates and supporting documents, BPSC typically accepts PDF or JPG format with a maximum size of 500KB per document. If your scanned certificate is larger than 500KB:

  1. If it is a JPG scan — upload to CompressPic Compressor and compress to 70-75% quality
  2. If it is a PDF — reduce the scan resolution to 150 DPI when scanning instead of 300 DPI
  3. Scan in black and white mode instead of colour for text documents — file size drops by 60-70%
